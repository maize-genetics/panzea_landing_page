See our [Data](/data) page for datasets snd genotype search tools.

All of the software we develop is open-source, and we encourage input to help make it better!

[TASSEL](http://www.maizegenetics.net/#!tassel/c17q9) is a Java software package to evaluate trait associations, evolutionary patterns, linkage disequilibrium, and more.

See also the [Genomic & Opensource Breeding Informatics Initiative (GOBii)](http://cbsugobii05.tc.cornell.edu/wordpress/)

[GAPIT](http://www.maizegenetics.net/#!gapit/cmkv) is our R software for relating genotype to phenotype with algorithms for genome wide association mapping.

Also take a look at our [Publications](/publications).

Or see v[ideos of one of our GBS workshops.](http://cit.webcast.video.cornell.edu/Mediasite/Catalog/catalogs/gbs_workshop_2013)
