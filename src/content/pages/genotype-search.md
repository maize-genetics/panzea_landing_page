This search tool can be used to obtain a subset of genotypic data from some of our large genotypic data sets. These data sets include maize HapMapV1 and V2, [MaizeSNP50 Bead Chip](http://www.illumina.com/products/maizesnp50_dna_analysis_kit.html) (aka 55K SNP array) genotypes for our "maize 282" association panel, and the 1144 traditional SNPs from our original NAM map. The search allows you to specify a chromosomal region and/or taxa (sample) subset of interest.

A similar search tool specifically for our GBS genotypes is available [here](/gbs-genotype-search).

Complete genotypic data sets (containing all chromosomes and taxa) are available for download as flat files [here](/genotypes).
