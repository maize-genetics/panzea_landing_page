In collaboration with the [Paleontological Research Institution's Museum of the Earth](http://www.priweb.org), we developed the [Teacher-Friendly Guide to the Evolution of Maize](http://maize.teacherfriendlyguide.org/). The TFG is designed to provide teachers with the background information needed to teach evolution, using maize as an example.

We also have a traveling [science museum exhibit](/museum-exhibit).

We hope to encourage young people to consider a [career in science](/careers-in-science). Also see our [women in science](/women-in-science) page.

See the National Science Foundation video "[Secrets of Plant Genomes Revealed!](http://www.nsf.gov/news/mmg/mmg_disp.jsp?med_id=61504)" starring some of our project members.

Bill Gates visited our group and others at Cornell University and wrote about it, including a video: [The Love Life of Plants](http://www.gatesnotes.com/Development/Love-Life-of-Plants-Cornell-University)
