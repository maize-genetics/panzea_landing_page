## Other maize diversity data from our collaborators:

Genomes2Fields

G2F is an umbrella initiative to support translation of maize genomic information for the benefit of growers, consumers and society. Several PIs of the grant are involved in this project. Environmental, phenotypic, and genotypic (GBS) data is available through their resources page:

- [Genomes2Fields](http://www.genomes2fields.org)

- [Data](http://www.genomes2fields.org/resources/data/)

SeeDs of Discovery

The Seeds of Discovery (SeeD) initiative is a platform for utilizing maize and wheat genetic resources, with lessons that can be applied to other crops. SeeD has put together tools to facilitate the efficient use of these data and the germplasm that they describe that can be accessed online:

- [About SeeD](http://seedsofdiscovery.org/about/)

- [SeeD Maize Germinate](http://germinate.seedsofdiscovery.org/maize/) (phenotypic, genotypic, and passport data)

- [Unimputed GBS](http://23.253.100.239/dvn/dv/seedsofdiscoverydvn/faces/study/StudyPage.xhtml?globalId=hdl:11529/10034) derived SNPs for maize landrace accessions represented in the SeeD-maize GWAS panel (Romero et al. 2017)

- [Beagle imputed GBS](http://23.253.100.239/dvn/dv/seedsofdiscoverydvn/faces/study/StudyPage.xhtml?globalId=hdl:11529/10035) derived SNPs for maize landrace accessions represented in the SeeD-maize GWAS panel (Romero et al. 2017)

- [Romero et al. 2017 GWAS results](http://hdl.handle.net/11529/10906)

- [Romero el al. 2017 GWAS code](https://github.com/jar547/FOAM_FloweringTime/blob/master/FT.model)

European diversity

Our European colleagues have different diversity datasets publicly available from recent publications

- [Unterseer et al.2016](https://figshare.com/articles/Data_from_Unterseer_et_al_2016_Genome_Biology/3427040/1) : Genotypic information of 70 American and European Dent and 66 European Flint inbred lines

- [Unterseer et al. 2017](http://biorxiv.org/content/early/2017/01/27/103747): European Flint reference sequences
