Women in the news:

Keiko Torii, professor of biology at the University of Washington (UW), chief editor of The Arabidopsis Book, wins the Japanese Saruhashi prize, for a female scientist in the natural sciences for her work in the mechanism of cell–cell communication and stomatal development in plants. Read [more](http://blog.aspb.org/2015/11/03/keiko-torii-wins-japanese-saruhashi-prize/).

Barbara McClintock

Barbara McClintock is one of the most famous women scientists, and a pioneer in maize genetics.

Read more about Barbara McClintock, at [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_McClintock), [the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences](http://www.pnas.org/content/109/50/20198.full), [Famous Scientists](http://www.famousscientists.org/barbara-mcclintock/), or her [National Library of Medicine profile](http://profiles.nlm.nih.gov/LL/).
