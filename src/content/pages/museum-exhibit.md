## Maize: Mysteries of an Ancient Grain

In collaboration with the Paleontological Research Institute's Museum of the Earth, we developed a traveling science museum exhibit exploring the science of why maize has been an important crop for thousands of years.

The exhibit is mobile and has traveled around the U.S.

See its current schedule [here](/exhibit-schedule).

If you are interested in the museum traveling to a locale near you, contact [Beth Stricker](mailto:ews63@cornell.edu?subject=Maize exhibit)

The exhibit consists of two 15' S-shaped panels, 4 interactives, 10 scientist biography panels, title panel, and items for cases.
