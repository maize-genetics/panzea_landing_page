## Our outreach for the general public

In collaboration with the Paleontological Research Institute's [Museum of the Earth](http://museumoftheearth.org/), we have a traveling science museum exhibit. See [more about the exhibit](/museum-exhibit) or [where it is and will be](/exhibit-schedule)!

Interested in a career in science? Find out [more](/careers-in-science) about the possibilities and read [biographies](/allpeople) of how our team members became scientists and why they love their jobs!

See members of our group in the National Science Foundation video "[Secrets of Plant Genomes Revealed!](http://www.nsf.gov/news/mmg/mmg_disp.jsp?med_id=61504)"

Bill Gates visited our group and others at Cornell University and wrote about it, including a video: [The Love Life of Plants](http://www.gatesnotes.com/Development/Love-Life-of-Plants-Cornell-University)

See our video on "Why I like being a scientist"!
